# Ten rounds of improvement (Amin, 18 Oct, night)

Each round: play, see what is weakest, fix it, check it in the browser and with the tests.

1. **The story on screen.** A world opens with "World 1" and one line of story; a detour says how many living flowers wait in it. The title card tells the story; the end card counts worlds and flowers.
2. **Monsters react.** The moment a monster sees or hears him, a ring of its eye-light opens from its head, so he knows he has been noticed.
3. **Flowers call to him.** Each living flower has a faint column of light over it, seen from afar. Picked, it flies up into the map circle, and the count there swells.
4. **The land comes back where he has been.** Where a flower stood, a patch of green grass grows, in colour above the dust, and it stays when he comes back from a detour.
5. **Sand underfoot.** Landing kicks up a small puff of dry sand; running leaves a little behind each step.
6. **Each detour is a place.** A detour leads to an oasis among the dunes, the old city of broken walls and towers, or a canyon of tall rock and cacti. From the road, a gate whose detour still holds a flower glows in the flowers' colour.
7. **Flowers are guarded.** The nearest monster that can reach a flower in the open (without crossing a pit) stands watch a little way off it, facing it. A flower is something to sneak to or fight for.
8. **Fights read better.** A hit or a defeat throws dark smoke instead of coloured petals, so the only flowers are the living ones. With no charges left, the charge marks shake. A broken stone throws shards that fall and fade.
9. **The pause card says where he is:** "World 1 · 3 of 10 flowers found".
10. **A full check.** Played through: the story lines, the map circle and the whole map, a flower picked and its grass growing, guards standing by the flowers (they had been left where they spawned: fixed), a detour into the oasis with its own map branch, the pause card. Tests and build pass.
