Privacy

Disposable Tricks knows nothing about you and keeps nothing about you.

No sign-in
There are no accounts. You are never asked for a name, an email address or a password.
No personal data
The app does not ask for any and does not collect any.
No cookies, no tracking
The app sets no cookies. It has no analytics and no advertising. It keeps only two things in your browser, on your own device, and never sends them anywhere: how loud you set the music and the sounds, and any game you press Save on, under "Saved". You can delete each saved game there, and clearing your browser's site data removes everything.
The game runs on your phone
The game itself runs in your browser. The way you play is not sent anywhere.
What goes to an AI while you make a game
While you make a game, the choices you have made so far and anything you write in the text field are sent to our server, which passes them to an AI service (Groq or Cloudflare Workers AI) to turn them into options and game settings. Nothing else is sent with them: no name, no account, no cookie. We do not store what you write and it is never part of a share link. The AI service receives the text and handles it under its own terms, so do not write anything personal. With the AI switched off, or when it does not answer, the app reads your words itself and nothing is sent.
The share link holds only the game
A link carries the settings of one game: its mood, scenery, monsters, weapon and twist, any shapes composed for it, its colours, its name and the number its level is built from. They sit after the # in the link, a part that browsers do not send to a server. The link says nothing about who made it.
Nothing from other companies
The pictures, sounds and fonts all come from this site. Your browser loads nothing from any other company; the AI service above is only ever reached through our own server.
What no website can avoid
To deliver these pages, the company that hosts them (Cloudflare) receives the internet address of the device asking, as happens with every page you open. The app itself never sees it and keeps no record of it.
Ages 16 and up
Disposable Tricks is made for people aged 16 and older.
